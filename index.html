Our mission

A gallerist's eye, working for your career.

muses muses exists because most talented artists don't fail because of their work — they fail because of how they present it, position it, and sell it.

I.

Elevate your work

An expert outside perspective on your work: what's working, what's diluting it, and how to present it with the strength it deserves.

II.

Strengthen your profile

Portfolio, biography, social media — we build a coherent profile that a collector or gallery takes seriously at first glance.

III.

Learn to sell yourself

Pricing strategy, positioning and communication: the business tools rarely taught in art school.
